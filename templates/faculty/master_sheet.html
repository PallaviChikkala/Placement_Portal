Automatic Email Notifications

When you upload an .xlsx or .csv master sheet, the system automatically:

  • Updates existing profiles — finds students by email or roll number and refreshes their CGPA, branch, phone, Aadhar, PAN, etc. Sends each student a "Profile Updated" email listing what changed.
  • Creates new accounts — if a student's email isn't registered, a new profile is saved in the database. Sends a "Welcome" email with login credentials (temp password = Roll Number).

 Make sure the sheet has columns: email, name, roll_number, cgpa, branch, batch, phone_number, aadhar, pan. Configure SMTP credentials in app.py to enable email delivery.
