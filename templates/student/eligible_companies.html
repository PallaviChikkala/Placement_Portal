NIT AP Placement Policy & Tier Rules

Please read the tier classification and restriction policy carefully before applying to any drives. Your eligible status is calculated automatically based on these rules.

Tier 1 Roles

Package: Above 15.00 LPA

Rule: High-priority placement category. Once selected, student is blocked from lower tiers.

Tier 2 Roles

Package: Between 7.00 LPA and 15.00 LPA

Rule: Mid-tier placement category. Students can only upgrade to Tier 1 roles.

Tier 3 Roles

Package: Below 7.00 LPA

Rule: Foundation placement category. Students can upgrade to Tier 1 and Tier 2 roles.

Policy Restriction: Students who are selected for a Tier 1 company cannot apply for companies in Tier 2 and Tier 3. Students selected in Tier 2 cannot apply for Tier 2 or Tier 3 (they can only upgrade to Tier 1). Students selected in Tier 3 cannot apply for Tier 3 (they can upgrade to Tier 1 and Tier 2).
